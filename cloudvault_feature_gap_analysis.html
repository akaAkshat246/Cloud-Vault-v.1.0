P0 — Critical gaps (build immediately)
Real-time collaborative editing
P0 · Critical
Every major competitor (Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox) supports live co-editing. Without it, CloudVault cannot serve teams at all. This is the single most important gap.
Built-in document editor
P0 · Critical
Google Docs-style editor with rich text, tables, and inline comments. Proton Docs (launched Dec 2025) now offers E2EE editing. CloudVault must match this baseline.
Team spaces & RBAC
P0 · Critical
No competitor lacks this. Owner / Admin / Editor / Viewer / Guest roles are table stakes for any B2B use case. All revenue growth depends on this screen being built.
Third-party integrations hub
P0 · Critical
Dropbox integrates with 1,500+ apps. Box offers SOC-certified integrations. Even a Zapier/webhook layer would unblock dozens of enterprise workflows immediately.
P2 — Moderate gaps (months 1–3)
FIDO2 / YubiKey (WebAuthn)
P2 · Moderate
Hardware 2FA is a security differentiator — especially alongside ZKE. Tresorit and Proton both offer it. High-value for enterprise and security-conscious users.
Document scanner with OCR
P2 · Moderate
Mobile-only scan-to-cloud feature. Dropbox has it. Makes CloudVault the capture point for physical documents. Pairs perfectly with AI auto-tagging.
Offline document editing
P2 · Moderate
Google Drive and OneDrive both support offline edits with sync-on-reconnect. Critical for mobile users in low-connectivity environments like India.
HIPAA / GDPR compliance center
P2 · Moderate
Tresorit is HIPAA/SOC 2/ISO 27001 certified. Box offers compliance on its base business plan. Without this, CloudVault cannot enter healthcare or EU regulated markets.
Zapier / Webhook automation
P2 · Moderate
Dropbox has native Zapier integration. Webhooks allow upload-triggered workflows (e.g., auto-send invoice to accounting). Low build effort, high B2B value.
Android homescreen widget
P2 · Moderate
Android 12+ Material You widget for quick file access. Google Drive has it. Strong engagement booster, especially in Android-dominant markets like India.
P3 — Low gaps (months 3–6)
Delta / block-level sync
P3 · Low
Dropbox's #1 technical differentiator — only changed file blocks are uploaded. Saves bandwidth dramatically on large files. Expected by power users.
Desktop sync client (Electron)
P3 · Low
File system integration on Windows/macOS. Dropbox and OneDrive both live in the OS file explorer. Without it, CloudVault is web/mobile only — limiting power users.
File comments & annotations
P3 · Low
Inline comments on file preview. Google Drive and Dropbox both support @-mention threads. Required for design review workflows.

These 12 features are not offered by any major competitor in their current form. Shipping even 3–4 of these would give CloudVault a category-defining edge.
Private E2EE AI assistant
No competitor has this
An AI that analyses your files without ever decrypting them server-side. Uses on-device inference or homomorphic-style processing. Proton just launched Lumo but it's still server-side. First mover advantage is real.
Lifetime storage plans
No big competitor offers
pCloud and Internxt offer lifetime plans (one-time payment for 500GB–2TB). Google, Dropbox, OneDrive, Proton, Tresorit — none do. Huge conversion driver for Indian price-sensitive market. Generates upfront cash.
AI predictive storage tiering
Unique to CloudVault
Auto-move cold files to archival tier with one-click restore. AI learns access patterns and proactively shows "You could save ₹340/mo by archiving these 47 files." CloudVault already has cost insight — extend it to automation.
AI-powered ransomware shield
Cutting edge in 2026
Monitor upload patterns for mass-encryption anomalies (ransomware signature). Freeze sync and alert user before vault is poisoned. Proton and Tresorit have passive ransomware resistance via E2EE; active detection is a step beyond.
Secure file request portal
Only Dropbox & Tresorit
Allow anyone to submit files to your CloudVault without having an account — and with E2EE throughout the transfer. Tresorit has file requests but not E2EE during submission. A major differentiator for legal, medical, HR use cases.
Encrypted eSignature
Dropbox Sign — but no E2EE
Dropbox Sign and DocuSign handle signatures but without zero-knowledge encryption. CloudVault could offer the world's first E2EE eSignature — all signature metadata encrypted client-side. Massive legal sector opportunity.
Multi-cloud backup vault
No consumer product offers
Connect Google Drive, Dropbox, or OneDrive to CloudVault and E2EE-backup everything into one encrypted vault. Users who don't trust Big Tech can use CloudVault as an encrypted layer on top. Huge privacy play.
AI legal & contract analyser
Unique AI vertical
Scan uploaded PDFs and contracts for key clauses, risk flags, expiry dates, and obligations. Store summaries alongside the encrypted file. Zero-knowledge — the AI runs on the decrypted file locally, never the server.
Data sovereignty controls
Enterprise differentiator
Let users and enterprises choose exactly which country their encrypted data is stored in (India, EU, US). With tightening data localisation laws (India DPDP Act, EU AI Act), this is a compliance advantage no consumer cloud storage offers.
Self-hosted / hybrid deployment
Nextcloud territory
Offer CloudVault on-prem for enterprises (like Nextcloud but with a polished UX and built-in AI). IT can run it on their own infra. Major enterprise deal-closer for banks, hospitals, and government bodies in India.
Encrypted video streaming
MEGA does partial
Stream E2EE video files without decrypting to a temporary file first. MEGA has basic encrypted video but it's slow and buggy. A smooth, YouTube-quality encrypted preview would be a genuine first and a huge media use-case win.
Agentic file organisation agent
2026 AI frontier
Let users describe a rule in plain English ("move all invoices from last quarter into Finance/2026/Q1 and rename them by date") and an AI agent executes it across the vault. No competitor has an agentic file management layer.
